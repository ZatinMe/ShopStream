I've completely transformed the ShopStream application to match Myntra.com's signature design with their distinctive fashion e-commerce styling.

### 🏠 **Header & Navigation**
- **Clean white background** with subtle shadows

- **Minimalist navigation** with clean typography

- **Size buttons** (S, M, L, XL) with hover effects
